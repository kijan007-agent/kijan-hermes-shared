# Medical Domain Criteria — Research Reference

## ME/CFS Diagnostic Criteria

### ICC 2011 (International Consensus Criteria)
- **Core:** PEM (Post-exertional Malaise) — mandatory
- **Mandatory:** ≥3 of 4: (1) Cognitive impairment, (2) Orthostic intolerance, (3) Unrefreshing sleep, (4) Pain
- **Plus:** ≥2 of 4: Neurological, Immune, Endocrine, Energy production
- **Duration:** ≥6 months
- **Exclusion:** Other causes ruled out

### IOM 2015 (Syndromic Diagnosis)
- **SEID:** ≥1 of 2: (1) Substantial new onset/definitive onset of PEM, (2) Fatigue new onset ≥6mo
- **Plus:** ≥1 of 2: (1) Orthostatic impairment, (2) Unrefreshing sleep
- **Symptoms:** ≥3 of 6: Cognitive, pain, tender points, nocturnal symptoms, immuno, neuro

### PEM — Patient Reality
- **Latency:** 6-48h after trigger (not immediate!)
- **Disproportionate:** Minimal activity → massive reaction
- **Duration:** Days to weeks recovery
- **Multiplicative:** 2x activity ≠ 2x crash, but 10x crash
- **Triggers:** Physical, cognitive, emotional — ALL count
- **Not psychological:** Objectively measurable via CPET

### ME/CFS — Comorbidity Matrix
| Condition | Overlap | Mechanism |
|-----------|---------|-----------|
| POTS | 30-60% | Autonomic dysregulation |
| Fibromyalgia | 30-60% | Central sensitization |
| hEDS | 10-30% | Joint hypermobility → venous pooling |
| MCAS/HIST | 20-40% | Mast cell activation, histamine |
| SFN | 15-30% | Small fiber neuropathy |

## Fibromyalgie — Diagnostische Kriterien

### ACR 2010 (Clinical Practice)
- **WPI** (Widespread Pain Index): 0-19 regions
- **SSS** (Symptom Severity Scale): fatigue + waking unrefreshed + cognitive symptoms (0-3 each)
- **Diagnosis:** WPI ≥7 AND SSS ≥5 OR WPI 3-6 AND SSS ≥9
- **Duration:** ≥3 months
- **Exclusion:** No other condition explaining pain better

### ACR 1990 (Classification)
- Diffuse pain in all 4 quadrants + axial spine
- ≥11 of 18 tender points at ~4 kg/cm²
- Points: Occiput(2), Trapezius(2), Supraspinatus(2), 2nd rib(2), Lateral epicondyle(2), Gluteus(2), Greater trochanter(2), Knee(2)

### Fibromyalgie — Central Sensitization
- **Pathophysiology:** Hyperactive central nervous system, "turns up the volume"
- **Neuroplastic:** Long-term potentiation in nociceptive pathways
- **Decreased inhibition:** Serotonergic/adrenergic descending pathways fail
- **Neurotransmitters:** ↑Substance P, ↑Glutamate; ↓Serotonin, ↓Norepinephrine, ↓Dopamine
- **Neuroinflammation:** Activated microglia (PET-confirmed)

### Fibromyalgie — Therapie-Referenz
| Drug | Dose | Mechanism | Notes |
|------|------|-----------|-------|
| Amitriptylin | 10-100mg | TCA, sleep + pain | Low start, slow titration |
| Duloxetin | 30-60mg | SNRI | Approved for chronic musculoskeletal pain |
| Pregabalin | 75-300mg | Ca²⁺ channel | Approved for FMS in many countries |
| Milnacipran | 50-100mg | SNRI | FMS-specific approval in some countries |
| NSAIDs | — | Anti-inflammatory | **Often ineffective for FMS pain** |

## POTS — Diagnostische Kriterien

### Diagnostic Criteria (Autonomic Society Consensus)
- **HR increase ≥30 bpm** (adults) or ≥40 bpm (12-19y) within 10 min standing
- **No orthostatic hypotension** (systolic ↓ <20 mmHg, diastolic ↓ <10 mmHg)
- **Symptoms:** Chronic orthostatic intolerance ≥3 months
- **Exclusion:** Other tachycardia causes ruled out

### POTS — Pathophysiology
| Type | Mechanism | Marker |
|------|-----------|--------|
| Hyperadrenergic | Sympathetic overdrive | NE >600 pg/mL |
| Hypovolemic | ↓ Plasma volume 10-20% | Albumin dilution test |
| SFN-associated | Small fiber damage | Skin biopsy |
| Post-viral | Immune autonomic dysfunction | History of viral trigger |

### POTS — Therapie
| Intervention | Dose/Detail | Mechanism |
|--------------|-------------|-----------|
| Salt loading | 10-12g/day | Volume expansion |
| Water loading | 2-3L/day | Volume expansion |
| Compression | Waist-high or knee-high | Reduce venous pooling |
| Beta-blockers | Propranolol/Atenolol | HR control |
| Midodrin | Alpha-1 agonist, daytime | Peripheral vasoconstriction |
| Fludrocortisone | Mineralocorticoid | Sodium retention |
| Pyridostigmin | AChE inhibitor | Modulate parasympathetic |
| Leveritt Protocol | Recine-first rehab | Exercise approach |

## Pain Assessment Tools — Quick Reference

### NRS (Numeric Rating Scale)
- 0-10 scale, single question
- Core measure for pain intensity
- Limitation: "10" is subjective per patient

### BPI (Brief Pain Inventory)
- Intensity: worst, least, average, current
- Interference: mood, walking, work, sleep, enjoyment
- **Track interference, not just intensity**

### McGill SF-MPQ-2
- 11 sensory + 4 affective items + PVAI + rating
- Multidimensional — best for clinical reports
- Too complex for daily tracking

### PainDETECT
- Neuropathic vs. nociceptive screening
- 9 items + pain diagram
- Quick filter for medication selection

### PROMIS Pain Measures
- PROMIS Pain Severity: 8-12 items, CAT
- PROMIS Pain Interference: 10 items, CAT
- T-scores with 50 = population mean

## Komorbiditäts-Matrix (Kijan-relevant)

```
ME/CFS ───┬── POTS (30-60% overlap)
          ├── Fibromyalgia (30-60% overlap)
          ├── MCAS (20-40%)
          ├── Histamine Intolerance (20-40%)
          └── SFN (15-30%)

Fibromyalgia ───┬── Central Sensitization (shared)
               ├── POTS comorbidity
               └── IBS/SIBO (common)

POTS ───┬── hEDS (up to 80% overlap)
        ├── MCAS
        └── ME/CFS-like fatigue
```

## Baseline Lab Panel (Ausschlussdiagnostik)
1. CBC (anemia, inflammation)
2. TSH, fT3, fT4 (thyroid)
3. HbA1c (diabetes)
4. Basic chemistry (kidney, liver, electrolytes)
5. CRP, ESR (inflammation)
6. Vitamin D, B12, Ferritin (deficiency)
7. ANA, RF (autoimmune screen)
8. EBV serology (if relevant)
